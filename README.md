# Executive Decision Intelligence (EDI)

## Confident decisions for uncertain times.

Executive Decision Intelligence is an AI-assisted decision analysis platform designed to help leaders evaluate investments, strategic initiatives, and uncertain business decisions.

## Vision

EDI combines:

- Financial analysis
- AI reasoning
- Uncertainty modeling
- Governance controls
- Decision traceability

The goal is not to replace executives.

The goal is to provide an intelligent challenge layer before important decisions are made.

## Current Capabilities

### Decision Analysis
- ROI evaluation
- Payback analysis
- Recommendation generation

### AI Decision Challenge
- Assumption questioning
- Executive questions
- Hypothesis generation

### Governance
- Decision trace
- Human approval controls

### Uncertainty Analysis
- Scenario testing
- Recommendation sensitivity
